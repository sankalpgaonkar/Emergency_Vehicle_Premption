How it decides

  1. Detect. Each analysed frame runs two YOLOv8 models: the project's emergency-vehicle model (ambulance / fire brigade / police) and a COCO model for ordinary vehicles.
  2. Confirm. One confident frame is not enough. An emergency vehicle is SUSPECTED at conf ≥ 0.35 and CONFIRMED only after 5 consecutive frames ≥ 0.50.
  3. Preempt. On confirmation: conflicting greens go yellow, then all-red, then the EV's approach turns green. After it crosses, signals recover to the normal cycle.
  4. Compare. The same arrivals are also run under plain fixed-time control, so you can see the EV delay with and without preemption (simulated).

Assumptions (read before quoting numbers)
  • Detection counts, confidences and timings are measured from the video by the models.
  • Everything in the intersection panel is simulated: 2 inbound lanes per approach, straight-through traffic, IDM car-following, fixed-time cycle 20 s green / 3 s yellow / 2 s all-red per axis.
  • The camera is assumed to be 90 m upstream of the stop line. Vehicles seen in the first frame start on the approach. Later vehicles enter at the time they first appear.
  • After the video ends, the video approach keeps the observed flow (capped at 30 veh/min).
  • The EV obeys signals in the simulation; a real EV may cross a red with caution.
